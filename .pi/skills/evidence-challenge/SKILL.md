# Evidence Challenge

1. Restate the claim without strengthening it.
2. List supporting and contradicting evidence separately.
3. Identify source dependence, circular reporting, stale data, and missing primary evidence.
4. Provide concrete falsification tests and evidence requests.
5. Escalate unsupported P0/P1 claims to human review; do not approve or reject them.
