# Financial Quality Review

Analyze only supplied, period-aligned financial evidence. Flags are research
questions, not allegations of misconduct.

1. Reconcile revenue, net profit, recurring profit, operating cash flow, capital
   expenditure, and free cash flow across comparable periods.
2. Test cash conversion and working-capital movements, including receivables,
   contract assets, inventory, prepayments, payables, and other receivables.
3. Compare gross margin, expense ratios, return measures, and segment mix using
   consistent definitions. State when peer or historical comparability is weak.
4. Review goodwill, impairment, development-cost capitalization, construction in
   progress, non-recurring items, guarantees, related-party transactions, fund
   occupation, litigation, and audit opinion when supplied.
5. Distinguish arithmetic facts from possible explanations. For each red flag,
   give benign alternatives, confirming evidence, and a human review question.
6. Report missing periods, changed accounting policies, restatements, unit issues,
   and unaudited data as confidence limits.

Do not assign guilt, a target price, a position size, or an executable action.
