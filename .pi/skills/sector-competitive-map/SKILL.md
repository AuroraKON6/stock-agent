# Sector Competitive Map

1. Define sector scope, value-chain boundary, covered companies, cutoff time,
   purpose, and the three to five operating metrics that actually describe the
   industry.
2. Separate market size, growth, regulation, technology, capacity, price, and
   demand claims. Attach supplied evidence references and distinguish measured
   data from estimates.
3. Map value-chain roles, customer and supplier concentration, barriers to entry,
   substitution risk, and where economics accrue.
4. Compare companies only on aligned periods, definitions, units, currencies,
   and consolidation scope. Show missing or estimated values explicitly.
5. Describe competitive evidence such as share change, utilization, unit cost,
   product mix, channels, order visibility, and customer retention when supplied.
6. Preserve competing explanations and identify data that could falsify each
   claimed advantage. Mark stale sector data and unsupported total-addressable-
   market claims.

Output a neutral research map, disagreements, uncertainties, and evidence gaps.
Do not rank investment opportunities or issue ratings, target prices, or trades.
