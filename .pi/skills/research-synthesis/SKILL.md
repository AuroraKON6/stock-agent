# Research Synthesis

1. Preserve each role's evidence and confidence.
2. State consensus and disagreements without averaging away material conflicts.
3. Distinguish facts, hypotheses, missing evidence, and next research tasks.
4. Route high-conflict or weakly grounded conclusions to human review.
5. Never convert research conclusions into executable portfolio actions.
